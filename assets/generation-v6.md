# Kitsu v6 artwork

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. All six PNGs preserve alpha transparency. Bed and feeder photos are shape/material references; the previous Kitsu preview is the character/style reference. Personal source photographs are not distributed.

The three carry atlases each contain 8 pickup poses and 8 walking poses. The meals atlas contains 4 lowering, 4 chewing, 4 raising and 4 awake front-facing resting poses. The dog, jaws and held toy are drawn together; runtime does not add a toy overlay to the walking muzzle. The feeder's falling pellets and remaining portion are animated in HomeObjects.cs. The bed front is composited over the pet during rest.
