# Kitsu 6.0.2 · mouth-grip posture animations

The dog, opened jaws and toy are rendered together in every frame; no foreground toy overlay is used for these transitions. Existing carry and chew atlases serve only as character, toy and endpoint references. No personal photographs are included.
